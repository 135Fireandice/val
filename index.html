Anatomy of a round

Every match is a race to 13 rounds. Each round repeats the same four beats, and the best teams win by managing the first one.

Buy phase

Spend credits on a weapon, armor and abilities before the gates open.

30 seconds

Take space

Attackers push toward a site while defenders hold angles and rotate on info.

Up to 100 seconds

Plant

Attackers drop the spike on a site. Now defenders are on the clock.

Spike burns for 45 seconds

Resolve

Defuse, detonate, or eliminate the other team. Credits roll into the next buy.

Win 13 to take the map

Operators

All 29 playable operators. Tap a card to see its four abilities. Role, difficulty and origin come from public guides. The four ratings are my own 1 to 5 estimates from each kit, because Riot doesn’t publish operator stats.

A new operator has been teased but isn’t playable yet, so it isn’t listed.

Ability effects

Every effect has its own colour. Tap one to fire it in the arena and repaint the whole site in its colour.

Tap anywhere in the arena to fire the selected effect

Inspect the spike

A fully 3D model built from CSS. Drag to spin it and tilt it, or let it turn on its own. It takes on whichever colour theme you pick.
